# Modeling algorithms

`apartment.ts` is a bounded layout compiler shared by Gemini and Ollama recipes. A compact `apartment_layout` action expands to exterior walls, 20 m² clear bedrooms, 150 mm partitions, a 1.2 m clear corridor, an 8 m² bathroom, open living/kitchen, doors, windows and floor. It supports one to six bedrooms, fixed room areas, total area, overall dimensions, footprint shapes, furniture and optional concept MEP. Apartments have no roof; houses add roofs.

`index.ts` supplies concept guidance across all supported elements: levels, walls, hosted doors/windows, floors/roofs, columns/beams, catalogue equipment, ducts, pipes and cable trays. Existing `playbooks` select relevant tools and catalogue entries; `recipes.ts` computes repeated coordinates. Equipment dimensions remain catalogue based; existing MEP connections require project evidence.

`allocation.ts` searches bedroom widths for compact proportions, preserves clear bedroom areas, reserves a 1.2 m corridor and partitions, and solves the remaining depth against the requested total internal area. A bathroom core can end before the rear wall, allowing living/kitchen space to wrap around it. Explicit living/dining, kitchen and bathroom areas remain fixed; additional shared area becomes dining/family space. Without an explicit total, the recommendation grows with bedroom and shared-room requirements. Infeasible requests produce a suggested area rather than silently shrinking requested rooms. `AiBuildingPresets` exposes the shared solver above the input; explicit template parameters work independently of conversation history.

Research (accessed 2026-09-17): [England's nationally described space standard](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/technical-housing-standards-nationally-described-space-standard/technical-housing-standards-nationally-described-space-standard) informs gross internal area and budgeting circulation. Its bedroom/dwelling minima depend on occupancy and storeys; 20 m² bedrooms and the chooser's below-11 m² warning / 18 m² recommendation are concept choices, not universal code minima. [Between 2 Courtyards House, architect-provided description](https://www.archdaily.com/954695/between-2-courtyards-house-eben) informs open living and private-room zoning. These are planning principles, not copied plans or a daylight/compliance claim.

`footprint.ts` validates rectangle, L, U or 3–16 freely drawn corners; width/length are internal spans. Automatic dimensions grow until the requested room areas fit. Fixed dimensions and total are reconciled, never silently ignored. An inscribed room block holds bedrooms/corridor/bathroom and stairs; additional polygon wings remain open shared space. Wall centrelines and slab edges offset the clear outline by half/full wall thickness. Concave duplex slabs are triangulated, clipped around the stair opening and merged, retaining all wings. This is a bounded concept layout, not a general circulation/daylight optimizer for every arbitrary polygon.

`furnishing.ts` packs catalogue beds/storage, kitchen sink/hob/cabinets/fridge, bathroom fixtures, sofa/tables into room bounds, reserving door swings and stair space. Dining chair geometry counts toward occupied bounds. Pieces that do not fit are omitted rather than overlapping; custom room sizes need a furnishing review. Services are optional: underfloor heating U-routes and supply/return trunks/manifold, cold/hot piping at floor/ceiling elevations, supply duct trunks/branches/diffusers/fan coil below ceiling. These are concept geometry, not complete connected or sized MEP systems. Houses default to an additional 3.5×6 m enclosed garage/car and 40 m² rear garden; carport/none, dimensions and garden area are configurable. Extras in Arch → Insert use native component selection/color/Undo. Garage panels travel from vertical through a bend to horizontal overhead tracks, controlled by opening percentage (stored in openingAngleDeg, 90 = fully open).

The chat chooser previews layouts and sends explicit structured parameters, bypassing provider inference even after earlier chat. New conversation resets chat/options and shows the current model inventory. Template builds consume zero provider tokens. Proposals remain limited to 150 input actions; deterministic expansion supports up to 400 elements, allowing furnished duplexes with services/outdoor extras to apply atomically.

Drawing workflow: PDFs up to 20 MB are rendered locally into a selected-page JPEG; images are resized before the 2.5 MB combined request limit. A locally served, deployment-traced PDF.js worker avoids a CDN dependency. Previews expose page selection and two normalized reference points with a known length, or total internal area. Gemini receives that calibration and page provenance with the image and is instructed to preserve the visible topology, use dimensions when readable, and ask one scale question when none is available. Clarifications retain the drawing for the next request. Scale from total area is approximate and based on the enclosed building outline, never the page rectangle.

`brief.ts` recognizes standalone residential requests, number words and common apartment spelling variants. Omitted bedroom counts mean two for apartments, three for villas/houses/bungalows, and five for duplexes. One to six bedrooms are supported. Prefix and suffix counts both work, for example “three-bedroom apartment” and “duplex house with five bedrooms”.

`house.ts` compiles `house_layout`: a villa is one storey with larger living space and a flat roof. A duplex is one two-storey dwelling with bedrooms split between aligned floors, a bathroom on each floor, slab solids forming a concept staircase, an upper-floor opening and a flat roof. Extra sleeping-zone bays are studies. Slab-built stairs are concept geometry; railings, native stair semantics and construction detailing are not included.

Standalone basic residential commands can compile locally with zero provider tokens. Attachments, conversation, selections and custom constraints go through the selected provider, which has access to the same recipes. Explicit dimensions always take precedence. All results pass the same strict geometry and reference validation before application. Defaults are concept choices, not regulatory standards.
